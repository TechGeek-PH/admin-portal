Urgent Additional Ticket — Admin Exception

By default, one active ticket is allowed per technician. For documented urgent operations only, Owner/Admin may authorize a second ticket for six hours. Time In remains mandatory; approvals are recorded, and this does not create wage entitlement or bypass ticket-level labor review.
